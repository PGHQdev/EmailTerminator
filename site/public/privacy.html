Privacy statement

Effective 28 September 2026

EmailTerminator is a desktop app. It reads your mail on your computer and keeps the results on your computer. We do not receive your mail, its subjects, its addresses or its sender domains. We have no user accounts.

What stays on your computer

The app stores what it finds in a local database. The database is encrypted. Mailbox passwords and sign-in tokens go into your operating system's secret store (Keychain, Credential Manager or Secret Service). To remove all of it, use "Erase local data" in the app.

Requests the app sends

The app sends only these requests. Each one is in the public source code, and you can build the app yourself to compare.

RequestSent toWhat it sends
Update checkGitHubA request for the latest version number. GitHub sees your IP address.
Your mailboxYour mail providerYour address and app password (IMAP), or a Microsoft sign-in (Outlook.com). The app reads mail and changes nothing in the mailbox.
Outlook.com sign-inMicrosoftA standard Microsoft sign-in. The app asks only for permission to read mail (Mail.Read), to stay signed in, and for your address.
UnsubscribeThe senderA one-click unsubscribe request, only when you choose to unsubscribe. It is the request the sender put in its own email.
Licence activation and checkemailterminator.comOnly on a licensed copy: the licence key and a hash of the computer's ID, at activation and every 15 days.
Cancel statsemailterminator.comOnly if you turn it on: the name of a known service, the action, the result and the week. Nothing for a sender that is not on the public service list.
AI modelThe endpoint you enterOnly if you set up a model. The app sends to that endpoint what the feature needs. The provider's own privacy terms apply.

The app sends no crash reports, no usage analytics and no advertising identifiers.

Microsoft data

For Outlook.com, the app uses Microsoft Graph with read-only access. The mail it reads stays on your computer. We do not sell, share or transfer data from Microsoft to anyone. You can remove the app's access at any time at account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Purchases

Polar sells the licence as merchant of record. Polar handles your payment and billing details under its privacy policy. We receive the order and the licence key from Polar.

What our server keeps

For each licensed computer: the licence key, the hash of its ID, and the dates of activation and of the last check. For cancel stats: the counts, with no key and no hash. You can free a computer from the app or from Polar's customer portal.

Website

This website has no cookies and no analytics. Cloudflare serves it and sees your IP address.

Changes and contact

We record changes to this statement in the repository. For a question, open an issue on GitHub.
